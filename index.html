FAQ

What is live now — and what is not.

Can I buy Pro today?

Not yet. Planned pricing is shown as a transparent demand test while the payment platform is under review. Early-access requests influence build priority and launch order.

What can I use today?

Agent Budget Router v0.1.0 is public on GitHub and provides deterministic local-first routing. The paid Control Plane is the product being validated next.

Does this replace Langfuse, Portkey or AgentOps?

That is not the goal. Those tools are strong in gateway and observability workflows. YJS AI Labs is focusing on orchestration before execution: context selection, model choice, quota awareness, handoff and ROI.

Will you support local models?

Yes. Local/cloud hybrid routing is part of the product thesis. The objective is to keep sensitive or low-value work local when policy and capability allow it.

Why GitHub Issues for early access?

The first users are expected to be AI builders and power users. GitHub requests give us structured demand evidence without waiting for the billing stack. Do not post secrets or confidential project data.
